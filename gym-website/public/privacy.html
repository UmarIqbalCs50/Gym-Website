Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

1. What We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your full name, email address, phone number (optional), and gender. We also store your selected membership plan and payment status.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to manage your gym membership, allow you to access workout and diet plans, and enable the gym admin to verify your payment. We do not sell or share your personal information with any third parties.

3. Password Security

Your password is never stored in plain text. It is encrypted using industry-standard bcrypt hashing before being saved to our database.

4. Email Communications

We may send you emails related to your account, such as password reset links. We do not send marketing emails without your consent.

5. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in an encrypted cloud database. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting the gym directly. Upon request, we will remove your information from our systems within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Cookies

This website uses browser storage (localStorage) to keep you logged in between visits. No third-party tracking cookies are used.

8. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the gym directly through the contact information on the homepage.
